Privacy Policy for Viofuse

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect via Google Services

When you connect your Google Account to Viofuse to utilize the synchronized media streaming features, our application requests access via official Google OAuth APIs to interface with your Google Photos library.

2. How We Use Your Data

Viofuse does not store your Google Photos files, images, or library metadata on our servers. All media data is processed on-device or streamed transiently during active shared viewing sessions.
